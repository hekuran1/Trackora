# Trackora

### Know what you own. Track what matters.

**Trackora is an asset lifecycle management platform designed to help organizations manage their assets, people, processes, and costs from one centralized workspace.**

From registering an asset to assigning it, maintaining it, auditing it, and eventually retiring it, Trackora aims to bring the entire asset lifecycle into one organized system.

Managing assets shouldn't mean juggling spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and incomplete records.

Trackora is being built to make asset management more visible, accountable, and efficient.

---

## Why Trackora?

As organizations grow, keeping track of equipment, ownership, locations, maintenance, software licenses, and asset costs becomes increasingly difficult.

Trackora aims to give organizations a centralized view of their assets and the information needed to manage them throughout their useful lives.

Whether managing IT equipment across departments or tracking physical assets across multiple locations, the goal is the same: better visibility, stronger accountability, and fewer disconnected workflows.

## What we're building

### 1. Centralized asset inventory

Maintain structured records of the assets an organization owns or manages.

- Asset registration and unique identification
- Asset categories and classifications
- Serial numbers, models, manufacturers, and purchase information
- Asset status and location tracking
- Ownership and assignment records
- Searchable, organized asset information

### 2. Asset assignments and accountability

Connect assets to the people, teams, and locations responsible for them.

- Employee and user assignments
- Asset check-in and check-out workflows
- Returns, transfers, and reassignment history
- Responsibility tracking
- Support for onboarding and offboarding workflows
- Visibility into who has which assets

### 3. QR codes and physical inventory

Bridge the gap between digital asset records and physical equipment.

- QR-based asset identification
- Asset labels and scanning workflows
- Physical inventory verification
- Record reconciliation
- Identification of missing or incorrectly recorded assets

### 4. Asset lifecycle management

Follow assets from acquisition to retirement.

- Acquisition and registration
- Deployment and assignment
- Transfers and status changes
- Maintenance and repair history
- End-of-life and retirement workflows
- Lifecycle records and historical visibility

### 5. Maintenance, warranties, and costs

Keep the information needed to manage asset upkeep and ownership costs organized.

- Maintenance and repair records
- Service history
- Warranty information and expiry dates
- Purchase and maintenance costs
- Lifecycle cost visibility
- Information to support repair-versus-replacement decisions

### 6. Software and license management

Extend asset management beyond physical equipment.

- Software inventory and license records
- License allocation and ownership
- Subscription and renewal tracking
- Expiry-date visibility
- License utilization and compliance support

### 7. Reporting, analytics, and audits

Turn asset records into useful operational information.

- Asset inventory and status reports
- Asset distribution by category, location, or department
- Assignment and lifecycle history
- Asset valuation and cost visibility
- Audit and inventory verification support
- Identification of idle, missing, or underutilized assets

### 8. AI-powered asset assistance

Explore a more intelligent way to interact with asset information.

The vision is to make it easier to understand asset records, identify relevant information, and answer operational questions without manually searching through disconnected records.

AI capabilities will depend on the final implementation, data availability, and access permissions.

### 9. Organizational workspaces and access control

Build on a SaaS architecture designed for organizations and teams.

- Separate organizational workspaces
- Role-based access control
- Administrative and member permissions
- Secure access to organizational data
- Workspace administration
- Subscription and usage management

Security, tenant isolation, and appropriate authorization are core architectural concerns, not optional extras.

## Built for practical asset management

Trackora is intended for organizations that need more control over the assets they own, use, assign, maintain, and manage.

Potential use cases include:

- IT departments managing computers and other equipment
- Businesses tracking equipment across teams and locations
- Organizations managing employee-assigned assets
- Operations teams coordinating maintenance and repairs
- Companies seeking better asset inventory and audit visibility
- Organizations managing physical assets alongside software licenses

The aim is to make asset management accessible and organized without unnecessary complexity.
